# Session Log — June 27, 2026

## Change of Plan
- **Parked story mode for now** — branching choose-your-path was too much work for one feature (needs art, animation, dialogue, and branching logic per hero). Hero cards stay on the menu as "coming soon."
- **Started Trivia Mode instead** — a self-contained, shippable feature we can get playable fast. Story mode is not deleted, just paused.

## What We Built — Trivia Challenge (framework + first questions)
- **Data model** — `Models/TriviaQuestion.swift`: id, question, 4 answers, correctIndex, explanation, plus `category`, `difficulty`, and `source` so the bank can grow and stay accurate.
- **Question bank** — `Data/trivia_questions.json`: 12 verified questions across 4 categories (Olympians, Heroes, Monsters, Myths & Stories) and 3 difficulties.
- **JSON loader** — `Data/Bundle+Decode.swift`: generic `Bundle.decode(...)` helper (reusable for `heroes.json` later).
- **Quiz logic** — `ViewModels/TriviaViewModel.swift`: an `ObservableObject` that shuffles the deck, tracks the current question, score, selected answer, and finished state.
- **Quiz screen** — `Views/TriviaView.swift`: progress bar, running score, tap-to-answer with green/red feedback, explanation + source, Next / See Results button.
- **Results screen** — `Views/TriviaResultView.swift`: themed rank by score (e.g. "Worthy of Olympus!"), Play Again, Back to Menu.
- **Menu wiring** — `Views/MainMenuView.swift`: added a "Trivia Challenge" card at the top that pushes the quiz.

## Verified
