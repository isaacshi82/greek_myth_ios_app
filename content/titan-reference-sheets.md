# The Twelve Titans — Reference-Sheet Prompts

**What these are:** one clean portrait of each Titan, made *first*, before any story
panels. You pick your favorite of each and keep it. Later, when a Titan appears in a
scene, you hand Gemini that saved picture as a **reference image** so they look like the
same person every time.

## How to use these
1. **Generate one Titan at a time.** Make 3–4 tries each, keep your favorite.
2. **Paste the whole prompt as-is** — the STYLE line and the FRAMING line are the same
   every single time. Don't reword them (that's what keeps the look consistent).
3. **Aspect ratio: square 1:1.**
4. Name your favorite `titan-oceanus`, `titan-coeus`, etc. (list at the bottom).
5. **Cronus and Rhea already exist in the app** — for those two, upload the existing
   art (from `content/chapter-01/` panels) as a reference image so they match.

> **STYLE (identical in all):** Bold graphic-novel illustration fusing the painterly
> cel-shaded art of the video game *Hades* (Jen Zee) with Japanese manga linework: strong
> inky black outlines, dramatic rim lighting and deep shadows, saturated jewel-tone
> palette, expressive angular character design, dynamic cinematic composition. No text, no
> speech bubbles, no watermark.

> **FRAMING (identical in all):** Character reference sheet — a **single** figure shown
> from head to waist in a calm, regal three-quarter pose, centered on a **simple dark
> gradient background with no scenery and no other characters**, clear dramatic lighting
> that shows the face and costume well. A majestic, **human-shaped** Titan (NOT a monster),
> tall and dignified, from the Golden Age before mortals.

## The six brothers (male Titans)

### titan-oceanus — Titan of the world-ocean
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Oceanus**, eldest of the Titans, the great river that circles the
> world: a mature, powerful, calm Titan with long flowing blue-green hair and beard that
> move like ocean currents, a circlet of shells and coral, deep teal and sea-green robes
> that ripple like waves, faint gentle glow of water around him. Wise, serene, oceanic.

### titan-coeus — Titan of the inquiring mind
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Coeus**, Titan of intelligence and the great axis of the heavens: a
> tall, lean, thoughtful Titan with short dark hair and sharp intelligent eyes, deep indigo
> and silver robes patterned with fine constellation lines, holding a slender staff topped
> with a single glowing star. Quiet, wise, questioning.

### titan-crius — Titan of the constellations
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Crius**, Titan of the stars and the seasons: a strong,
> broad-shouldered, proud Titan with curved ram's-horn motifs on his circlet, autumn-gold
> and bronze armor decorated with star-charts, a dark cloak scattered with glowing
> constellations. Firm, martial, steady.

### titan-hyperion — Titan of heavenly light
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Hyperion**, Titan of light and father of the Sun, Moon, and Dawn: a
> handsome, noble, radiant Titan with golden hair like rays of sunlight, warm glowing skin,
> gleaming eyes, a crown of light, warm gold-and-amber robes, a soft halo of dawn light
> around him. Bright, warm, majestic.

### titan-iapetus — Titan of mortal life and craft
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Iapetus**, Titan of mortality and craft, father of Prometheus and
> Atlas: a rugged, weathered, strong Titan with dark hair streaked with grey, earthen-red
> and iron-grey garments, strong calloused hands, holding a long spear. Grounded, resolute,
> work-worn and dignified.

### titan-cronus — the youngest Titan, who becomes king
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Cronus**, the youngest and boldest Titan: tall and imposing, sharp
> jaw, intense dark eyes, long dark hair, dark earth-toned armor, holding a great curved
> gleaming adamantine **sickle**, a fierce and darkly ambitious expression. Dangerous,
> commanding.
>
> *Consistency: match the Cronus already drawn in chapter1-04 / chapter1-06 — upload one of
> those as a reference image so his face and armor stay the same.*

## The six sisters (Titanesses)

### titan-theia — Titaness of sight and shining light
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Theia**, the far-shining Titaness of light and sight, mother of the
> Sun, Moon, and Dawn: a graceful, luminous Titaness with long shimmering gold hair,
> bright gleaming eyes, glowing gold-and-white flowing robes, a soft radiant aura. Warm,
> serene, brilliant.

### titan-rhea — the mother of the gods
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Rhea**, Titaness of motherhood, wife of Cronus and mother of the
> Olympians: a dignified, nurturing Titaness with long flowing dark hair, warm gentle
> features, a draped robe in deep red and gold, a subtle lion motif, calm and protective
> strength. Kind, regal.
>
> *Consistency: match the Rhea already drawn in chapter1-07 / chapter1-09 / chapter1-10 —
> upload one of those as a reference image so she stays the same.*

### titan-themis — Titaness of divine law and order
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Themis**, Titaness of divine law, order, and fairness: a stately,
> upright Titaness with neatly bound hair, a calm and just expression, robes of deep blue
> and bronze, holding a set of perfectly balanced scales. Dignified, fair, serene.

### titan-mnemosyne — Titaness of memory
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Mnemosyne**, Titaness of memory and mother of the nine Muses: a
> gentle, dreamy Titaness with long wavy hair and thoughtful faraway eyes, soft violet and
> silver robes patterned with faint flowing ripple-lines like a stream of memory. Calm,
> reflective, quietly wise.

### titan-phoebe — Titaness of prophecy and the moon
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Phoebe**, the bright Titaness of prophecy and the moon, keeper of
> Delphi: a luminous Titaness with silver-white hair, pale glowing skin, a crescent-moon
> circlet, robes of silver and midnight blue, holding a laurel branch, a mysterious knowing
> gaze. Lunar, oracular, otherworldly.

### titan-tethys — Titaness of fresh water
> *[STYLE] [FRAMING]* **Tethys**, Titaness of fresh water, wife of Oceanus and mother of the
> rivers: a gentle, nurturing Titaness with flowing blue-green hair adorned with river-reeds,
> robes like cascading water in aqua and pale blue, soft caring expression, little droplets
> and gentle currents around her. Calm, flowing, motherly.

## After generating
Name your favorites `titan-oceanus` … `titan-tethys`, then add each with:
`./add-asset.sh ~/Downloads/<file>.jpeg titan-oceanus references` (etc.)

Keep them all — these are the **reference images** you'll feed back into Gemini whenever a
Titan shows up in a future story panel, so the whole family stays consistent.
