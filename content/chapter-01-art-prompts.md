# Chapter 1 — Art Prompts ("The Birth of the Gods")

**Style:** Hades (video game) × Japanese manga · **Aspect ratio:** square 1:1 (set in Gemini's UI) · **Output names:** `chapter1-01` … `chapter1-15`

## How to assemble each prompt
`FINAL PROMPT = [STYLE BLOCK] + [Character Bible entry for each character in the panel] + [Scene]`

Paste those three pieces together for every image. Keep the STYLE BLOCK and Bible entries **word-for-word identical** every time — that repetition is what keeps the look consistent.

## How to guarantee consistency (do these in order)
1. **Lock the Style Block** — paste it verbatim on all 15. Never paraphrase it.
2. **Build character reference sheets first.** Before doing scenes, generate one clean portrait of each recurring character (Hermes, Cronus, Gaia, Rhea, Zeus). Pick the best of each = your "canon."
3. **Use Gemini's reference-image feature.** Feed the canon portrait back in ("use this exact character") when generating each panel they appear in. This is the single biggest consistency lever — the latest Gemini image model is strong at holding a character across images.
4. **Reuse exact descriptors.** The Bible entries below use fixed, specific wording (hair, palette, clothing). Specificity = consistency. Don't rewrite them per panel.
5. **Work in one chat thread per character.** Conversational image models keep context: establish Cronus, then say "same Cronus, now seated on a throne…".
6. **Batch by character**, not by panel order — do all the Cronus panels in a row off the same reference.
7. **Fix the palette & lighting language** (jewel tones, rim light) — it's baked into the Style Block so every panel shares a mood.

- **Hermes (guide):** a youthful god with a late-teen look, lean and athletic, tousled dark hair, bright mischievous eyes and a sly grin; winged sandals, a short belted tunic with a small traveler's cloak, golden accents.

## Panels (characters + scene — prepend STYLE BLOCK + bible entries)

**chapter1-01** · *Characters: Hermes* — Hermes mid-stride, turning to the viewer, gesturing to follow, before a swirling starry void. Warm golden key light, motion.

**chapter1-02** · *Characters: none* — A vast dark cosmic void: swirling mist and faint sparks of light, endless empty space. Moody deep blues and blacks, lonely and primordial.

**chapter1-03** · *Characters: Gaia, Uranus* — Gaia the Earth-goddess rising from a landscape of mountains and forests below; Uranus the star-formed Sky-god arching vast above her. Epic, reverent, the first king and queen of all.

**chapter1-04** · *Characters: Cronus (young), the Titans* — A group of twelve towering, regal Titans standing across a primordial landscape; the young Titan Cronus steps forward, confident. Awe-inspiring scale, low heroic angle.

**chapter1-05** · *Characters: Gaia* — A sorrowful Gaia; trapped beneath her glowing surface are monstrous shapes — one-eyed Cyclopes and hundred-handed giants — locked inside the Earth. Dramatic underground glow, oppressive.

**chapter1-06** · *Characters: Cronus (young), Gaia* — The young Cronus grips a giant gleaming curved sickle with a determined glare; Gaia hands it to him from the shadows. Tense, heroic rim lighting.

**chapter1-07** · *Characters: Cronus (king), Uranus (ghostly)* — Cronus the king seated on a massive ancient stone throne, crowned; the sky dims behind him and a faint ominous ghostly face of Uranus forms in the clouds. Foreboding.

**chapter1-08** · *Characters: Cronus (king)* — Extreme close-up of Cronus's worried, paranoid face lit by eerie cold light; glowing prophetic symbols swirl around his head. Unsettling, psychological.

**chapter1-09** · *Characters: Cronus (king), Rhea* — A giant shadowy Cronus cradling a single swaddled, glowing infant — stylized and mythic, like a dark fairy tale, NOT gory or frightening; Rhea weeps in the background. Dark, somber.

**chapter1-10** · *Characters: Rhea, Zeus (baby), Cronus (king)* — Rhea secretly cradles the glowing baby Zeus while holding out a swaddled stone toward a looming Cronus. Tender light on the baby, heavy shadow on Cronus. Tense, hopeful.

**chapter1-11** · *Characters: Zeus (baby)* — A lush hidden cave on a Greek island; the faintly glowing baby Zeus tended by gentle nymphs and a goat. Warm, safe, magical, secret.

**chapter1-12** · *Characters: Zeus (young god), Cronus (king), Poseidon, Hades* — A triumphant young Zeus as five adult gods (Poseidon with trident, shadowy Hades, and others) burst out in shafts of light from a staggering Cronus. Action, energy, golden light.

**chapter1-13** · *Characters: Zeus (young god), the Titans* — Epic battle across a storming sky: the young gods versus the giant Titans, Zeus hurling crackling thunderbolts, hundred-handed giants flinging boulders. Cinematic chaos, electric.

**chapter1-14** · *Characters: Zeus, Poseidon, Hades* — Three victorious gods standing over a glowing divided world: Zeus wreathed in lightning (sky), Poseidon with trident (sea), Hades in shadow (underworld). Triumphant, balanced, regal.

**chapter1-15** · *Characters: Hermes* — Hermes winking and tossing a glowing laurel toward the viewer, golden Mount Olympus rising behind him. Upbeat, inviting, victorious.
