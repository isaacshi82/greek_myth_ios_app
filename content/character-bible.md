# Character Bible — keeping gods consistent across the whole app

As more chapters arrive, the same gods reappear. To keep them recognizable:

## The workflow (do this once per character)
1. Generate **one canonical front-facing bust** from the reference prompt below; pick your favorite. That's the **canon**.
2. Save it (e.g. `assets/raw/characters/gods/zeus-ref.jpeg`).
3. **Every time** that god appears in a panel, (a) paste their one-line description, and (b) attach the canonical image as a **reference** in Gemini.
4. Rely on the **signature color + prop** so they read instantly even if details drift.

## Shared framing for every reference sheet
> *[STYLE BLOCK]* **Front-facing character reference bust, head and shoulders, facing the viewer, centered and symmetrical, neutral confident expression. Flat solid teal background. Square 1:1.**

## The six children of Cronus (Zeus's siblings)

### Zeus — *signature: white + gold, lightning*
> A regal young-to-middle god with dark curly hair and a short dark beard, piercing eyes, a white chiton with gold trim, faint crackling lightning around him. Commanding and heroic.

### Poseidon — *signature: sea-green/teal, trident*
> A rugged, powerful god with a wild teal-green hair and beard, weathered sea-toned skin, blue-green robes with wave and barnacle motifs, holding a golden trident. Stormy and proud.

### Hades — *signature: pale skin, dark shadow robes*
> A pale, somber god with dark hair, regal but grim, in a dark robe patterned with faint stars and shadow; cold dark wisps drift around him. Quiet, brooding, kingly.

### Hera — *signature: royal red/purple, peacock + diadem*
> An elegant, proud queen-goddess with a golden diadem, rich deep-red and purple robes, and peacock-feather motifs (eye-spotted feathers). Regal and a little severe.

### Demeter — *signature: gold/green, wheat*
> A warm, motherly goddess with auburn hair wreathed in golden wheat, a gold-and-green robe, holding a sheaf of wheat. Kind, earthy, abundant.

### Hestia — *signature: warm, veiled, hearth flame*
> A gentle, serene goddess, modestly hooded or veiled in soft warm cloth, with a small glowing hearth flame near her hands. Calm, humble, comforting.

## Note for chaotic group panels (e.g. chapter1-12)
In a busy action burst, don't expect all gods perfectly rendered. Lead with **Zeus + 2 clearly-signatured siblings** (Poseidon's trident, Hades' dark cloak) and let the rest read as "the siblings." Save the precise versions for calmer panels where each god is the focus.
