# Chapter 1 — The Birth of the Gods

**Unit:** The Beginning · **Quiz at the end:** "Titans & Creation" (already built)
**Guide:** Hermes · **Length target:** ~10–12 min · **Format:** illustrated narrated chapter (tap to advance)

> This doubles as the **art shot list**. Each panel has the narration the kid reads/hears,
> plus the exact image prompt to generate in Gemini. Keep every prompt's style anchor:
> *"Art style: animated fantasy illustration, reminiscent of Hades game meets Disney Hercules. No text, no watermark."*
>
> Tone: dramatic + funny, kid-safe. Accurate to Hesiod's Theogony. Hermes is the cheeky narrator.

---

### Panel 1 — Hermes intro (hook)
**Narration (Hermes):** "Hey — Hermes here, messenger of the gods, fastest feet on Olympus. Before there were gods throwing lightning and heroes slaying monsters, there was… nothing. Want to know how it all started? Keep up."
**Art prompt:** Hermes, a young god with winged sandals and a friendly grin, gesturing as if inviting the viewer in, standing before a swirling starry void. Warm golden key light. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 2 — Chaos
**Narration:** "First, there was Chaos — a great, yawning emptiness. Not a monster, not a place. Just… the beginning of everything."
**Art prompt:** A vast dark cosmic void, swirling mist and faint sparks of light, a sense of endless empty space. Moody, deep blues and blacks. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 3 — Gaia and Uranus
**Narration:** "Out of Chaos came Gaia — the Earth herself. And above her rose Uranus, the starry Sky. Earth and Sky became the first king and queen of all there was."
**Art prompt:** A majestic goddess made of green earth, mountains, and forests (Gaia) below, and a vast star-filled sky-god figure (Uranus) arching above her. Epic, reverent. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 4 — The Titans are born
**Narration:** "Their children were giants — the twelve Titans. Powerful, ancient beings who ruled before the gods you know. Their leader-to-be? The youngest: Cronus."
**Art prompt:** A group of twelve towering, regal Titan figures standing on a primordial landscape, one young confident Titan (Cronus) standing forward. Awe-inspiring scale. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 5 — Uranus the cruel father
**Narration:** "But Uranus was a fearful father. Some of his children were strange and strong — the one-eyed Cyclopes, the hundred-handed giants — and he locked them away deep inside the Earth. Gaia was furious."
**Art prompt:** A sorrowful giant Earth-goddess (Gaia) with monstrous shapes (Cyclopes, hundred-handed giants) trapped glowing beneath her surface. Dramatic underground glow. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 6 — Cronus rises
**Narration:** "So Gaia made a plan. She forged a great curved blade — a sickle — and asked her children: who's brave enough to stop their father? Only Cronus stepped up."
**Art prompt:** Young Titan Cronus gripping a giant gleaming adamantine sickle, determined expression, his mother Gaia handing it to him in shadow. Tense, heroic lighting. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 7 — Cronus takes the throne
**Narration:** "Cronus overthrew his father and became king of the cosmos. For a while, it was a golden age. But Uranus left him with a chilling warning…"
**Art prompt:** Cronus seated on a massive ancient stone throne, crowned, the sky dimming behind him, a faint ominous ghostly face of Uranus in the clouds. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 8 — The prophecy
**Narration (Hermes):** "'Just as you overthrew me,' said Uranus, 'one of YOUR children will overthrow YOU.' …Yikes. Cronus did not take that well."
**Art prompt:** Close-up of Cronus's worried, paranoid face lit by eerie light, prophetic words swirling as glowing symbols around his head. Dramatic, unsettling. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 9 — Cronus swallows his children
**Narration:** "So Cronus came up with a terrible solution: every time his wife Rhea had a baby, he swallowed it whole to keep it from ever growing up. One by one — five little gods, gone."
**Art prompt:** A giant shadowy Cronus figure holding a swaddled glowing infant, dramatic but NOT gory or scary — stylized and mythic, like a dark fairy tale. Rhea weeping in the background. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 10 — Rhea's plan
**Narration:** "When her sixth baby — Zeus — was born, Rhea had had enough. She hid the baby away and handed Cronus a stone wrapped in baby blankets instead. He swallowed it without even looking."
**Art prompt:** Rhea secretly cradling baby Zeus while holding out a swaddled stone to a looming Cronus. Hopeful, tender light on the baby; shadow on Cronus. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 11 — Zeus grows up in secret
**Narration:** "Far away on the island of Crete, baby Zeus grew up in a hidden cave, safe and strong, waiting for the day he'd return."
**Art prompt:** A lush hidden cave on a Greek island, baby/young Zeus glowing with faint power, nymphs and a goat tending him. Warm, safe, magical. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 12 — Zeus frees his siblings
**Narration:** "Grown and ready, Zeus returned — and tricked Cronus into coughing up the brothers and sisters he'd swallowed: Hades, Poseidon, Hera, Demeter, and Hestia. Fully grown, and furious."
**Art prompt:** A triumphant young Zeus standing as five adult gods emerge in bursts of light from a staggering Cronus. Action, energy, golden light. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 13 — The Titanomachy
**Narration:** "What followed was a TEN-YEAR war — the Titanomachy. Zeus freed the Cyclopes, who forged him his greatest weapon: the thunderbolt. The hundred-handed giants hurled boulders. The whole world shook."
**Art prompt:** Epic battle scene: young gods vs giant Titans across a storming sky, Zeus hurling crackling thunderbolts, hundred-handed giants throwing boulders. Cinematic chaos. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 14 — Victory
**Narration:** "The gods won. The defeated Titans were cast into Tartarus, the deepest pit. Then Zeus, Poseidon, and Hades divided the world: the sky, the sea, and the underworld."
**Art prompt:** Three victorious gods (Zeus with sky, Poseidon with sea/trident, Hades with shadowy underworld) standing over a glowing divided world. Triumphant, balanced composition. *(style anchor)*

### Panel 15 — Hermes outro → quiz
**Narration (Hermes):** "And THAT is how the age of the gods began. Not bad for a story that starts with a whole lot of nothing, right? Think you were paying attention? Prove it."
**Art prompt:** Hermes winking and tossing a glowing laurel/coin toward the viewer, Mount Olympus golden behind him. Inviting, upbeat. *(style anchor)*
**Action:** Button → launches the **Titans & Creation** quiz.

## Notes for production
- 15 panels × one illustration each = 15 images. (Generate in batches; keep Cronus/Zeus/Hermes consistent by reusing descriptors.)
- Narration can ship as **text first**; add AI/recorded voice-over later as a Phase 2 upgrade.
- Every story beat here is **covered by a question** in the Titans & Creation quiz (sickle, swallowed children, the stone, Cyclopes' thunderbolts, Titanomachy, the twelve Titans, the Hecatoncheires) — so the quiz genuinely tests what the chapter taught.
- Age-safety: the "swallowing" is framed mythically (swallowed whole, rescued later) — no gore.
